About

A bit about me

I got my first computer when I was 15, and I've been taking things apart to see how they work ever since. That curiosity turned into a Computer Science degree at Mansoura University, and somewhere along the way it turned into a genuine love for numbers, patterns, and the stories hiding inside messy spreadsheets.

I actually started out on the front-end side — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, the whole "make it look right" mindset. I still use that instinct every day, but these days most of my time goes into Python, SQL, Tableau, and Power BI, cleaning up datasets and turning them into dashboards that a non-technical manager could actually read at a glance. Certificates from Google's Data Analytics and IT Support programs, Meta's Front-End track, and hands-on Python work all fed into that shift.

What I'm chasing now is a remote data analyst role with a company that ships real products, not just reports. I do my best work under a deadline, I don't get rattled easily, and I'd rather spend a weekend debugging a broken join in SQL than watch TV — which probably tells you everything you need to know about me.
